A little help for your hotel

Welcome guests, care for their rooms and grow your countryside hotel at your own pace.

Need a hand?

Getting started

Guests arrive and check in automatically. Your team cleans used rooms and can handle guest requests. Follow the next-step card for small improvements, room looks and expansion goals. Hotel cash is fictional game currency.

Camera and pause

Drag to pan, pinch or use + / − to zoom, and use the camera controls to return to a close view or the map. Tap Pause to stop hotel time. You can clean a floor mess or repair a sink while paused.

Guest care

A guest may occasionally feel unwell and leave a small floor mess. Tap the mop bubble for a free cleanup. At 1× speed there is about a 30-second cleanup window before passing guests may notice it. A guest who notices an overdue mess shows a sad face and may leave a lower checkout review.

Bathroom repairs

After 12 completed stays, occasional bathroom sinks need care. Tap the wrench bubble to repair a leak for free. At 1× speed the repair window is about 45 seconds. The affected room's awake guest may notice an overdue fault. Open Guests or the room card to find an offscreen task.

Purchases and room collections

The opening chapter is free. Optional purchases include the Full Hotel unlock, faster hotel speeds, a Starter Bundle, hotel cash and room collections. Current localized prices appear in the app before Apple's purchase sheet.

After buying Botanical Retreat or Grand Atelier, Room 101 receives the collection. Use Boutique → your owned collection → Choose rooms, or Room → Room collection, to apply it elsewhere. Original room styling is always free to restore; earned furniture remains yours.

Missing or interrupted purchases

Use Restore purchases in Boutique or Support & privacy for permanent unlocks and collections, using the Apple Account that purchased them. Cash packs are consumables and are saved with their receipt in the local hotel wallet; they are not a separate cross-device cash account. An interrupted verified delivery retries when the app can save successfully. Keep the app installed while a delivery is pending, check free device storage and reopen the game. Contact support if it still does not arrive.

Apple handles billing and refund requests. See Apple's refund instructions.

Keeping your progress

Your hotel is saved locally. There is no game account or developer-operated cloud save. Uninstalling the app removes local progress and unused local hotel cash. Apple device backups may include app data depending on your device settings. Permanent purchases can be restored through Apple.
