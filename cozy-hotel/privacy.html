Privacy policy

Cozy Hotel Tycoon by Josh Kirouff

Effective October 24, 2026.

Your hotel stays on your device

The game stores your chosen hotel name, hotel progress, settings, room preferences and purchase-delivery records in its local app storage. The hotel name is used on your hotel's sign and does not need to be your real name. The game has no player accounts, social features or developer-operated cloud save, and does not send your hotel data to us.

Apple purchases

Optional in-app purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles your Apple Account, payment information, billing and its purchase verification services. The game receives verified product and transaction information to unlock purchased features and deliver hotel cash. Product ownership and transaction identifiers are stored locally to restore permanent purchases and prevent repeated cash grants. We do not receive your payment-card details or operate a purchase-data server.

Apple's handling of its services is described in Apple's privacy policy. Apple may retain purchase records according to its policies.

No advertising or analytics

The game does not include advertising, third-party analytics or cross-app tracking, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photo library. Game artwork and audio are bundled with the app. No tracking permission is requested.

Support and website links

If you choose to email support, we receive the email address and information you voluntarily send so we can respond. Do not send sensitive information that is unnecessary for your request. Support emails are used to handle the request and retained only as needed to resolve it, meet legal obligations or document an ongoing issue. You can request deletion by contacting the address below.

Support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening these pages sends ordinary web-request information, such as your IP address and browser details, to the host. These pages have no analytics scripts, advertising or contact forms. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Your local hotel data remains on your device until you delete the app or remove its data. Deleting the app removes local progress and local cash; it does not remove Apple's purchase records. Permanent purchases can be restored with the purchasing Apple Account. Apple device backups may retain app data according to your device and backup settings; the developer does not manage those backups.

There is no game account to delete, and you can play without sending us personal information. To ask about or delete information you sent directly to support, email us. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a child has sent personal information to support, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion.

Policy changes

Updates will be posted here with a revised effective date. Material changes to how the game handles data will be reflected in the app and its store disclosures.
